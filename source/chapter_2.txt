“So the Geng family is really like this?” Chen Ping’s eyebrows knitted together. He couldn’t believe that Geng Shanshan would really be like this.
Back when he was arrested, Geng Shanshan had been crying, saying she would wait for him to get out and that the two of them would get married!
How had it turned out this way? Chen Ping decided to find Geng Shanshan and ask her for an explanation.
But at that moment, the door of the room was suddenly rapped hard — so hard that it almost shook the door off its hinges!
Hearing the knocking, Tang Hongying’s face turned pale. It was obvious she was very scared.
“Mum, who is this?”
Looking at the expression on Tang Hongying’s face, Chen Ping asked with a frown.
“Don’t worry about it. Hurry back inside and don’t come out!”
Tang Hongying pushed Chen Ping into the room, then went to open the door, her face nervous!
The door had only just opened when a bald man walked in with four or five men covered in tattoos, their faces fierce.
“Is the money ready?”
Bald Head asked, giving Tang Hongying a look.
“Brother Bald Head, it’s all ready, it’s all ready …”
Tang Hongying kept nodding, reaching out and fumbling to take a cloth bag from the corner!
By now many of the neighbours had gathered around too. A few of them looked at Bald Head, the rest kept out of sight.
“These men come to ask for money every month. They really want to force people to death!”
“No, there’s no law about it at all!”
“Shh, keep your voices down. These men are sent by the Xiao family to collect money regularly.”
A few neighbours spoke angrily among themselves, but no one dared to do anything about it!
At that moment, the bald man grabbed the cloth bag from Tang Hongying’s hand and opened it to take a look.
“What the f*ck is all this stuff?” The bald man frowned and turned the cloth bag over. Worn-out banknotes scattered all over the place — hundreds, fifties, and one- or two-dollar bills, and even many coins of only a few cents!
“Can this pile of rags be worth ten thousand?”
Bald Head questioned Tang Hongying loudly.
“Brother Bald Head, it’s exactly ten thousand. We’ve counted it all. You can count it yourself if you don’t believe me.”
Tang Hongying went along with it, smiling and nodding her head.
“Bullsh*t!” Bald Head kicked Tang Hongying in the abdomen, knocking her straight to the ground. “Let me count? I don’t have that kind of time. Change it all into hundreds for me.”
“Mum!” Chen Ping scurried out of the inner room and hurriedly helped Tang Hongying up!
His eyes were cold as they swept across the bald men. A cold aura flashed in his eyes!
The bald men froze, and all of them shivered under Chen Ping’s gaze!
“Chen Ping, who told you to come out? Go inside quickly and stay out of it!”
Tang Hongying desperately pushed Chen Ping back towards the room!
“Mum, since I’m out here anyway, let me handle this. Just sit tight!”
Chen Ping helped Tang Hongying sit down on the stool, then turned and looked coldly at Bald Head.
Bald Head, on the other hand, gave Chen Ping a glance and said with a sneer on his face, “Isn’t this the guy who hit Young Master Xiao with a brick and spent three years in jail? I didn’t expect him to be out!”
“It’s really time for you to come out. Today is the big wedding day of your girlfriend and Young Master Xiao. Aren’t you the ex-boyfriend coming to attend?”
“Green hat turtle …”
“Hahahaha …”
Bald Head and a few of his junior brothers all burst out laughing!
“What did you say?”
Chen Ping frowned, his eyes filled with disbelief.
“I said that the woman you went to jail for is getting married to Young Master Xiao today. The wedding is being held at the Regal Hotel. It’s luxurious, aren’t you coming to see it?”
Bald Head looked at Chen Ping with a sneer on his face.
Chen Ping’s frown tightened and his hands clenched into fists.
Behind him, Tang Hongying’s face kept changing, and she was shaking with anger.
How could that woman — for whom her own son had gone to prison — turn around, follow her enemy, and get married?
“Kneel down and apologise to my mother, and I can spare your lives.”
A cold aura flashed in Chen Ping’s eyes as the killing intent rose up from his body.
The temperature inside the room suddenly dropped by a few degrees, and the laughter of several of the bald men came to an abrupt halt!
A moment later, Bald Head reacted, and said with an angry face, “What did you say? You want me to kowtow and apologise?”
With that, Bald Head smashed his fist at Chen Ping.
For Chen Ping’s skinny frame, that punch should have knocked him to the ground!
Boom …
But just as Bald Head rushed in, Chen Ping kicked out!
Bald Head’s entire body suddenly fell to the ground clutching his crotch, sweating profusely in pain and wailing continuously!
“Chen Ping, you can’t keep fighting …”
Listening to Bald Head’s wailing, Tang Hongying hurriedly yelled at Chen Ping.
Chen Ping had been in jail for striking someone. He had only just come out. If he was sent back in for fighting, how could he bear it?
“Beat him to death, beat him to death …”
Bald Head roared, glaring at Chen Ping with eyes full of malice!
Several of Bald Head’s men rushed towards Chen Ping.
Chen Ping glanced at his mother and flicked his hands. Flashes of white light flickered, and those men all felt their legs go numb and fell to their knees!
At this moment, the bald man was shocked, looking at Chen Ping with disbelief, and a chill rose up in his heart!
Those neighbours outside also stared at the scene before them in disbelief!
“Apologise to my mother!”
Chen Ping’s icy voice rang out again!
The bald man hesitated for a moment, but looking at that cold look in Chen Ping’s eyes, he could only kneel down on the ground.
“I’m sorry …”
Bald Head led a few of his men and knelt to apologise!
“Get lost!” Chen Ping waved his hand!
He did not want to kill anyone in front of the neighbourhood and his own mother. If he wanted to kill these few punks, it would only be a matter of snapping his fingers.
The bald man was helped to his feet by his men and gave Chen Ping a vicious look as he limped away, obviously still resentful at heart. But Chen Ping wasn’t afraid of the bald man’s revenge!
